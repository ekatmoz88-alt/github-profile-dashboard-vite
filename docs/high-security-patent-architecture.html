High-Security Work Patent
System Architecture Case Study

01 · PROJECT SCOPE

Proposed end-to-end system

The concept combined a high-security plastic identity card with an embedded dual-interface chip, application software, application and personalization infrastructure, dedicated readers and connections to external information systems.

Identity document

  • ID-1 plastic card based on laser-sensitive polycarbonate
  • Visual personalization on both sides by laser engraving
  • Embedded chip with contact and contactless interfaces
  • Proposed identity and work-authorization data set

Electronic applications

  • Structured personal and document data on the chip
  • Identity verification and data-reading scenarios
  • Proposed biometric capabilities: photograph and fingerprints
  • Payment application based on ПРО 100

02 · SYSTEM CONTEXT

Proposed process and participating systems

Application intake and card issuance point (ППВ)
↔
PPO Territory
Decision processing
→
Personalization centre

The presentation describes the following sequence: the intake point submits applicant data to PPO Territory for a decision on issuance or refusal; PPO Territory then sends the approved card data to the personalization centre; the finished card is returned to the intake point for delivery to the applicant.

Conceptual portfolio reconstruction from the presentation's process description and subsystem inventory. It is not a source-authored deployment diagram and does not imply that every connection shown was implemented.

03 · ARCHITECTURAL DOMAINS

Subsystems and cross-cutting concerns

Personalization infrastructure

  • Automated applicant photography
  • Laser graphic personalization and demetallization
  • Chip encoding and electronic application personalization
  • Quality control and card production equipment

Security and trust services

  • HSM cryptographic services
  • Key management for PIN envelopes and card personalization
  • Administration and authorization modules
  • Protected communications via VIP-Net

Card reading and field operations

  • Contact and contactless chip readers
  • Specialized autonomous readers
  • Mobile terminals with network connectivity
  • Proposed fingerprint capture and comparison

External applications and services

  • Federal-level information exchange
  • Payment application ПРО 100
  • Proposed electronic public-service access
  • Potential links to tax, healthcare and transport services

04 · SYSTEM ANALYSIS CONTRIBUTION

Analytical scope evidenced by the presentation

  • Analysis of the end-to-end issuance and circulation process for a secure identity document.
  • Identification of participating organizations, systems, hardware devices and operational roles.
  • Decomposition of the proposed solution into information-system, personalization, security, payment and field-reading domains.
  • Analysis of data sets stored visually on the card and electronically in the chip.
  • Definition of interaction scenarios between intake points, PPO Territory, the personalization centre and external agencies.
  • Identification of project workstreams and unresolved decisions concerning requirements, delivery organization, funding, stakeholders and external agreements.
