## Writing rules

- Prefer concrete deliverables over generic claims.
- Explain why important information-architecture decisions were made.
- Do not claim ownership of software implementation when the contribution was documentation or analysis.
- Do not imply production testing, stakeholder approval or engineering sign-off without evidence.
- Do not invent reductions in support requests, onboarding time or other business metrics.
- Keep confidential information out of public examples.
